⚙️ Setup Instructions in Kite Developer Console
  1. Log in to developers.kite.trade and create a Kite Connect app.
  2. Set the Redirect URL to: http://localhost:3000/api/kite/callback (or your VPS domain).
  3. Enter your API Key and API Secret above and click Save.
  4. Click Connect Zerodha Kite every morning at 9:00 AM to establish the live binary WebSocket stream.
🔒 Privacy Assurance: Zero trader tracking. Kite is exclusively used as a 1-way inbound market quote feed. Your client accounts, orders, positions, and ledgers remain 100% private in local MySQL.
